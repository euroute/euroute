<https://euroute.app> is the official hosted Euroute service, operated by the
Euroute project. No other deployment is official.

## What the software licence does not grant

- The name "Euroute"
- The Euroute logo, symbol, wordmark, favicon and visual identity
- Any suggestion of endorsement by, affiliation with, or official connection to
  the Euroute project or the hosted service

These brand assets are included in the repository so the application can be
built and run as-is. Their presence is not a grant of trademark or brand rights.

## What you may do

- Inspect, use, modify and redistribute the source code under AGPL-3.0-only,
  including running it as a network service, provided you meet the licence's
  source-availability requirements.
- Run a private or personal build for yourself, unchanged, to evaluate or
  develop the project.
- Refer to Euroute by name factually — for example "a fork of Euroute" or
  "based on Euroute" — as long as it is clear that your project is not Euroute
  and not the official service.

## What you may not do

- Present a fork, derivative work or deployment as the official Euroute service.
- Operate a public service under the Euroute name, logo or visual identity.
- Use the Euroute brand in a way that could confuse travellers about who is
  running the service they are using.

If you publish a fork under its own public identity, rebrand it: choose your own
name, logo, favicon and wordmark, and replace the brand assets in this
repository accordingly. Everything else — the code — is yours to use under the
AGPL.

## Notes

No claim is made here about registered trademark status. This document states how
the project asks its name and brand to be treated; it is not legal advice.

Questions about brand use can be raised as an issue in the repository.
